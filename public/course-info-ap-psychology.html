What we work through

The course is designed to make a large subject feel connected. Lessons can follow the AP syllabus, respond to current schoolwork, or focus on the areas that need the most attention.

01

People and behaviour

Learn how psychologists explain development, personality, social behaviour, and the ways people understand themselves and others.

02

Mind and biology

Connect the brain, sensation, perception, learning, memory, and cognition so the ideas are easier to recall and apply.

03

Evidence and application

Work with research methods, psychological perspectives, and exam-style questions without losing sight of the underlying ideas.

How a lesson works

Each session has a clear purpose, but the starting point can change. We use the time to make the next piece of the course more manageable.

01 / START

Find the sticking point

Begin with a topic, question, piece of homework, or exam area that needs attention.

02 / WORK

Make the idea usable

Break down the material, test the understanding, and connect it to examples and evidence.

03 / LEAVE WITH

A clearer next step

Finish with a short recap and a practical sense of what to review or practise next.
