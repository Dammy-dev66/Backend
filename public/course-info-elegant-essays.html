Elegant Essays

A guided writing course for developing essays that are thoughtful, distinctive, and clear without losing the writer’s own voice.

Course snapshot

Format
Tutor + one student, or tutor + two students
Best for
Students shaping personal, persuasive, creative, or high-stakes writing
Lesson style
Conversation, close editing, experimentation, and revision
Next step
Choose a lesson format and find a time that works

What we work through

The course treats good writing as both an act of thinking and an experience for the reader, with attention to meaning, movement, and voice.

01

Purpose and voice

Clarify what the piece needs to say and find a voice that feels natural, precise, and suited to the reader.

02

Shape and movement

Build paragraphs and transitions that carry the reader through the ideas with a sense of direction.

03

Language and finish

Refine word choice, rhythm, emphasis, and detail so the final piece feels considered rather than overworked.

How a lesson works

Each session has a clear purpose, but the starting point can change. We use the time to make the next piece of the course more manageable.

01 / START

Find the sticking point

Begin with an idea, draft, paragraph, or piece of writing that needs a closer look.

02 / WORK

Make the language work

Test the shape and voice of the piece, then explore changes that strengthen its effect.

03 / LEAVE WITH

A clearer next step

Finish with a practical direction for revising, extending, or completing the work.
