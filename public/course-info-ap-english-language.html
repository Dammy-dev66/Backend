What we work through

Lessons connect the choices a writer makes with the effect those choices have on a reader, then turn that understanding into purposeful written responses.

01

Reading and rhetoric

Read nonfiction closely and identify how purpose, audience, evidence, structure, and language work together.

02

Argument and evidence

Build a position, select useful evidence, and develop a line of reasoning that remains clear from beginning to end.

03

Writing under pressure

Plan and shape timed responses with more control, precision, and confidence in the writing process.

How a lesson works

Each session has a clear purpose, but the starting point can change. We use the time to make the next piece of the course more manageable.

01 / START

Find the sticking point

Begin with a passage, prompt, essay, or exam area that needs attention.

02 / WORK

Make the thinking visible

Unpack the text or argument, test the reasoning, and build a clearer response.

03 / LEAVE WITH

A clearer next step

Finish with a short recap and a practical focus for the next piece of practice.
