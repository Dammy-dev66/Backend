Essay Writing

Choose a lesson format and find a time that works

What we work through

The focus is on making the writing process easier to see, so each paragraph has a purpose and the whole essay feels connected.

01

Planning and structure

Turn a prompt or set of ideas into a workable argument, shape, and sequence before drafting.

02

Evidence and clarity

Use examples purposefully and make the explanation around them precise, relevant, and easy to follow.

03

Revision and voice

Strengthen sentences, transitions, and rhythm while keeping the writing recognisably your own.

How a lesson works

Each session has a clear purpose, but the starting point can change. We use the time to make the next piece of the course more manageable.

01 / START

Find the sticking point

Begin with a prompt, plan, paragraph, or draft that needs attention.

02 / WORK

Make the writing usable

Develop the idea, test the structure, and work through the changes that will have the most effect.

03 / LEAVE WITH

A clearer next step

Finish with practical feedback you can use in the next section, paragraph, or draft.
