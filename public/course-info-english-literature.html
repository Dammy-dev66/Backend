What we work through

The course makes room for both the detail of individual passages and the larger patterns that shape a text across a whole work.

01

Close reading

Explore imagery, voice, form, structure, and language so that observations become useful interpretations.

02

Ideas and context

Connect character, theme, setting, and context without allowing background information to replace the text itself.

03

Comparative writing

Shape clear, supported responses that compare texts thoughtfully and keep the argument in view.

How a lesson works

Each session has a clear purpose, but the starting point can change. We use the time to make the next piece of the course more manageable.

01 / START

Find the sticking point

Begin with a passage, question, essay, or text that needs a more careful look.

02 / WORK

Make the idea usable

Test interpretations, connect evidence to meaning, and develop a stronger written response.

03 / LEAVE WITH

A clearer next step

Finish with a short recap and a practical focus for the next piece of reading or writing.
